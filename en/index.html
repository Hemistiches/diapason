The name

A diapason, or tuning fork, gives the A: a fixed note, the same for everyone, to which every instrument tunes.

For health data, that note is written down: the General Data Protection Regulation, the French Public Health Code, the reference methodologies of the CNIL, the French data protection authority. It is the same for a pharmaceutical company, a digital health start-up or a clinic.

The test measures the gap. It does not ask whether you are compliant. It looks at what a supervisory authority asks to see, and at what, in your organisation, nobody is in charge of.

What the test reviews

Appointing a data protection officer is not enough. The officer must also be identifiable by the authority, free in the performance of their tasks and given the resources to perform them. This is where most set-ups give way: not in intention, but in structure.

  • Appointment of the officerMandatory where the core activities involve large-scale processing of health data (Art. 37 GDPR).
  • Independence and resourcesNo conflict of interests with other duties, and the time the role requires (Art. 38).
  • Contact details sent to the CNILThe simplest obligation, and one of the most often missing (Art. 37(7)).
  • Record of processing activitiesThe first document requested in an inspection (Art. 30).
  • Impact assessmentsRequired where the risk is high, which is common with health data (Art. 35).
  • Research and studiesCNIL reference methodology or CNIL authorisation: the legal basis sets the timetable.
  • Certified hostingA host holding a certificate of conformity (Article L. 1111-8 of the French Public Health Code).
  • Processors and transfersData processing agreements, safeguards outside the European Union.
  • Algorithms and AIA model trained on health data calls for a legal basis, an impact assessment and contracts with its data sources.
  • Personal data breachesA procedure that makes it possible to notify without undue delay and, where feasible, within seventy-two hours (Art. 33).

Is your set-up in tune?

Questions about facts you know: your organisation, what you have in place, your studies, the pace of your projects. The path adapts to your answers and asks for no documents or file details.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to start with a consultation or an assessment?

No. The orientation consultation is useful if you do not know where to start; it is never required. The assessment is for when your set-up exists but its soundness is uncertain. If your record and procedures already hold, the data protection officer subscription can start directly.

Can I entrust a study or a device without taking a subscription?

Yes. Each study, like each device, is a separate fixed-fee engagement. If a subscription exists or starts later, the work done is carried over: entering it in the record falls under the engagement, updating it under the subscription, with no double invoicing.

Do I need a compliance management tool?

No, it is not an obligation: the regulation requires up-to-date documents, not software. But I prefer to work in a dedicated tool: record, impact assessments and requests in one place, up to date and shared between you and me, which makes everyone's work simpler. If you already have one, I work with it. Otherwise, I suggest Provacy, a French publisher, hosted in the European Union, at a preferential rate of €1,500 excl. VAT per year, which you subscribe to directly with the publisher. Your data remains yours: you keep it if we stop working together. I help you get started with the tool and fill it in, as part of my engagement and counted as such; technical questions are for the publisher, which hosts the data in Germany and France. The tool only holds compliance documentation, never health data: its host is not certified for it. The firm receives no remuneration from the publisher.

Our device incorporates AI: where do we start?

With the data. Training or running an algorithm on health data falls under the GDPR today: legal basis, reuse of data, impact assessment, patient information, contracts with data sources. That is the scope of Diapason.

The device itself falls under another offer: Concordance, the firm's offer dedicated to medical device compliance ↗. It covers CE marking and the obligations of the AI Regulation, including those for high-risk systems embedded in a product, postponed by Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. It is the same lawyer: work done for one is carried over to the other, never invoiced twice.

How is the subscription level chosen?

According to the actual workload: how often new projects start, data subject requests, ongoing studies and their complexity, the number of entities, whether someone in your organisation can act as relay. Neither the number of studies, nor that of employees or countries, sets it on its own. The test gives a first indication, confirmed in a conversation. A one-off overrun does not change the level.

How are hours counted and carried over?

Every task carried out for you uses up the month's volume, including periodic reviews and preparation of the annual report. You receive a monthly statement and an alert before the volume runs out. One, two or three unused hours depending on the level move to the next month, are used first and expire at the end of that month. Beyond that, an overrun is carried out only with your agreement, at €180 excl. VAT per hour.

What happens in the event of a CNIL inspection or a data breach?

The officer remains the CNIL's point of contact and cooperates with it; initial advice and guidance fall within the subscription volume. If the situation calls for exceptional mobilisation, I propose an additional budget, in stages if need be. A pre-authorised envelope of hours, provided for in the fee agreement, makes it possible to act without waiting for your one-off agreement. I guarantee neither a standby service nor immediate availability.

How long am I committed for?

The proposed initial term is six months, the time needed to set up the follow-up programme; the engagement then continues for an indefinite term, with one month's notice on either side. You remain free to end it earlier: the months performed, notice included, and the overruns you accepted are then payable. No monthly fee is due beyond that, and no rate is recalculated.

At the end of the engagement, I hand over the record and documents, up to date, and you declare the end of the appointment, or the new officer, on the CNIL's online service. The exact terms are set out in the fee agreement.

Can the data protection officer also defend you before the CNIL?

That is an engagement separate from the data protection officer role. It is accepted only after checking that it is compatible with that role and that there is no conflict of interests, in light of your file.

Does the test result commit me?

No. It is an indicative orientation, based solely on your answers: neither legal advice nor a contractual offer. The scope and price are confirmed in a conversation, then in a signed fee agreement.
